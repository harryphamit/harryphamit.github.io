01 / about

Infrastructure, identity and automation

I'm an IT Specialist based in Toronto with hands-on experience across Windows Server, Azure, Exchange Online and hybrid Active Directory environments, backed by strong PowerShell automation and multi-client infrastructure operations.

My work covers the full lifecycle: designing and implementing server, identity and network infrastructure, then automating the administrative workflows around it so systems stay reliable and teams stop repeating themselves. Firewall migrations, Windows 11 rollouts, mailbox and identity administration, onboarding pipelines — delivered with zero production downtime.

That range comes from working at three very different scales: an MSP juggling nine tenants at once, an enterprise manufacturer with users across five countries, and field IT deploying hardware at bank branches and retail sites. A software engineering degree underneath it means the automation side comes naturally.
